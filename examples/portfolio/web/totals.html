The maintained query

The result is a regular Postgres table. Book totals are computed by Pgderive. The net-units chart and shown-book summary are calculated in this browser. Engine measurements and historical graphs come from the server.

SELECT book_id,
       COUNT(*) AS positions,
       SUM(quantity) AS units
FROM public.demo_positions
GROUP BY book_id;
